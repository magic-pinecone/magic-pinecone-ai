# 國立中央大學環境科技博士學位學程/遙測科技碩士學位學程學位論文品質與管考準則

國立中央大學環境科技博士學位學程/遙測科技碩士學位學程
  學位論文品質與管考準則
Guideline for Thesis Quality and Examination for Ph.D Program in
Environmental Science and Technology/Master of Science Program
  in Remote Sensing Science and Technology, National Central
  University
  本準則追溯適用所有在學學生
  This criterion applies retroactively to all current students

第一條 國立中央大學環境科技博士學位學程/遙測科技碩士學位學程（下稱本學程）
  為維護研究生之學位論文品質，符合學術倫理規範，依本校「精進學位論文
  品質實施要點」及「博士班、碩士班研究生學位考試細則」，訂定「國立中
  央大學環境科技博士學位學程/遙測科技碩士學位學程學位論文品質與管考
  準則」（下稱本準則）。
Article 1 The Ph.D. Program in Environmental Science and Technology/ Master of Science
  Program in Remote Sensing Science and Technology (referred to as "this
  Program") at National Central University aims to maintain the quality of graduate
  thesis, adhere to academic ethics standards, therefore establish the "Guidelines for
  Thesis Quality and Examination for the Ph.D. Program in Environmental Science
  and Technology/ Master of Science Program in Remote Sensing Science and
  Technology, National Central University" (referred to as "the Guideline"), in
  accordance with the "NCU Guidelines for Improving Thesis Quality" and
  “National Central University Rules Governing the Master’s and Doctoral Degree
  Qualifying Exam and Thesis Defense”
第二條 本學程研究生之論文指導教授資格依本校「學則」第 56-2 條規定辦理。每
  位專任(案)教授每學年新收指導研究生人數上限以 5 位為原則。二位教師共
  同指導者，每位研究生以 0.5 位計。
  前項如有異議，由本學程學術委員會議協調之。
Article 2 The qualification of thesis advisors for graduate students in this Program are in
  accordance with Article 56-2 of the NCU Academic Regulations. Each full-time
  professor (including project professor) may generally accept up to 5 new graduate
  students per academic year. In cases where two professors jointly supervise a
  student, each professor is counted as 0.5 towards this limit.
  Any disputes regarding the above shall be resolved by the Academic Committee
  of this Program through consultation.
第三條 本學程研究生之學位論文須符合本學程專業領域，並經指導教授及學位考試
  委員確認，如有不符本學程專業領域之爭議，依本校「精進學位論文品質實
  施要點」第七條規定辦理。
Article 3 The thesis of graduate students in this Program must align with the professional
  field of this Program. It requires confirmation from the thesis advisor and the
  degree examination committee. If any dispute that does not conform to the
  professional field of this program, procedures will be conducted in accordance
  with Article 7 of "NCU Guidelines for Improving Thesis Quality."
第四條 本學程研究生辦理學位口試申請、口試進行、畢業離校程序前，須繳交定稿
  學位論文之「論文相似度比對報告」，且排除引言、參考文獻、目錄及附件，
  其比對相似度應低於百分之二十五。如未符合所訂標準，須敘明具體理由並
  經指導教授確認，始得辦理離校手續。
Article 4 Postgraduate students in this program must submit a "Thesis Similarity
  Comparison Report" of the finalized degree thesis before applying for the degree
  oral examination, conducting the oral examination, and graduating from school.
  This report should exclude the introduction, references, table of contents, and
  appendices, and the similarity index should be below 25%. If it does not meet this
  standard, specific reasons must be provided and confirmed by the thesis advisor
  before proceeding with the graduation procedures.
第五條 學位考試委員，應對學位候選人之研究領域有專門研究，口試委員資格依本
  校「博士班、碩士班研究生學位考試細則」第四條認定。如依第四條第二目
  之 3、4 特殊條件遴聘口試委員，須提交委員之專長領域與學生學位論文內
  容之相關性說明，送本學程學術委員會議核定遴聘資格。
Article 5 The examination committee members shall have specialized research expertise in
  the research field of the degree candidate. The qualification of oral defense
  committee members shall be determined in accordance with Article 4 of the
  “National Central University Rules Governing the Master’s and Doctoral Degree
  Qualifying Exam and Thesis Defense”. If committee members are appointed under
  the specific conditions outlined in Article 4, Section 2, items 3 and 4, a justification
  of the relevance of the committee members' expertise to the content of the student's
  thesis must be submitted. This submission shall be reviewed and approved by the
  Academic Committee of this Program for appointment qualification.
第六條 學位論文依「學位授予法」第十六條規定，應提供公眾於國家圖書館館內閱
  覽紙本，或透過獨立設備讀取電子資料檔，但涉及機密、專利事項或依法不
  得提供，並經學位考試委員認定者，得申請學位論文不公開或延後公開。
Article 6 According to Article 16 of the Degree Conferral Act, the thesis should be made
  available for public access either in printed form at the National Central Library or
  through independent electronic devices. However, if the thesis involves
  confidential matters, patents, or is legally restricted from public access, and upon
  recognition by the degree examination committee, the student may apply for the
  thesis to be kept confidential or for delayed public access.
第七條 本準則經學術委員會議及中心會議通過後，自發布日施行。
Article 7 These guidelines shall be effective from the date of publication after approval by
  the Academic Committee meeting and the Center's meeting.
