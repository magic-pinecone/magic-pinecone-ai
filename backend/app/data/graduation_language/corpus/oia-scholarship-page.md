❓ FAQ - Application Related
Q
If my scholarship period has already passed the maximum duration, do I still need to apply?
A
YES! You still need to submit the application. Even if your scholarship period has overed, you are still eligible to apply for
Tuition and Credit Waiver
.
Q
Where can I apply for a transcript, and which type should I apply for?
A
You can apply for your transcript at 2 locations:
(1) Self-service Machine near to Zhi-Dao Lecture Hall. (Across from the OIA)
(2) Office of Registrar, 3rd floor of the Teaching & Research Building.
Please apply for a
full academic year transcript
. (Either Chinese or English is acceptable)
Q
If I plan to return in the upcoming semester, do I need to apply for the scholarship?
A
YES! You still need to apply according to the scholarship application schedule for the semester you plan to return.
(1) Return in Spring semester: Apply
before February 1st
.
(2) Return in Fall semester: Visit OIA website
before June 5th
for details.
Q
Can I use the Chinese course I took during my master's degree as the Chinese Proficiency Requirement while applying for the scholarship for PhD degree?
A
No! As master's and PhD programs are separate levels, NCU scholarship requirements must be fulfilled independently for each program. PhD students are still required to take a Chinese course during their current program.
❓ FAQ - Disbursement Related
Q
Are there times when the monthly stipend might be delayed?
A
In general, the monthly stipend will be DELAYED: (1) in the first month of each semester, (2) during the Lunar New Year month. Processing time may also vary depending on administrative schedules.
Q
If I apply for suspension, withdrawal or graduation, will it affect my monthly stipend?
A
Yes! If you complete the procedure before the 15th of the month, the scholarship for that month and any following months will not be issued. If the procedure is completed after the 15th, the scholarship for that month will still be issued, but no further payments will be issued.
Q
If I only receive a tuition/credit waiver, is there anything I should be aware of?
A
Even with a tuition/credit waiver, miscellaneous fees will still apply. Please log in to Portal to download your payment slip, you should then pay for the remaining miscellaneous fees.
