 Article 1  The Study Regulations for the Doctoral Program of the Department of
  Electrical Engineering at National Central University (hereinafter
  referred to as the Regulations) are stipulated per National Central
  University Regulations on the Degree Examinations for Postgraduate
  Students.
 Article 2  Students have to obtain the approval of their thesis advisors for choices
  on course selection and leaving school. Students who have not yet
  chosen their thesis advisors have to obtain the approval of the convener
  of their respective division for the said choices. To reach the number of
  course credits required for graduation, a doctoral student must earn at
  least 18 course credits, and a postgraduate admitted via direct admission
  to the doctoral program must earn at least 34 (including at least 18
  course credits from the doctoral program). Before graduation, students
  must take the course "Seminar" for at least six semesters; any students
  who wish to be exempted from taking the course due to any special
  reasons must seek approval from the Academic Committee, and the
  students in question can be exempted from taking the course if their
  exemption is approved by the committee.
 Article 3  Each student must submit a Thesis Advisor Commitment Form within
  one month after the first school day in the first semester since he or she
  is enrolled, and his or her thesis advisor should be a full-time faculty
  member at the Department of Electrical Engineering. If any student
  needs to choose a thesis advisor at any other department or institute, the
  student must submit an application in written form to the Academic
  Committee for review and approval; moreover, the student in question
  must have a co-advisor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at
  National Central University (NCU). A student should not change his or
  her thesis advisor during his or her thesis-writing process. If a student
  must replace his or her thesis advisor or add any new co-advisor due to
  any reasons, the replacement or addition may be granted if the student's
  original advisor agrees to either of them first and if the Academic
  Committee approves the student's written application for the
  replacement or addition submitted to the Department of Electrical
  Engineering.
Article 4  The qualification and procedure for graduation are as follows:
  1. A student must pass the subjects of the qualification examination
  specified by his or her division (relevant matters should be handled
  per the regulations of the qualification examination stipulated by
  each division). Students may take the qualification examination after
  they enroll in the doctoral program, and they have to pass all subjects
  by the end of the fifth semester after their first enrollment. If they fail
  to pass all the subjects, they will be forced to drop out of school. The
  qualification examination will be held twice a year, and a student can
  pick the number of subjects of examinations for each qualification
  examination.
  2. A student must pass the qualification review of his or her division and
  that of the Academic Committee.
  (1) A student must publish at least two academic papers in any SCIE
  journal, and both papers have to be related to his or her doctoral
  thesis. Or, a student may publish one academic paper in a journal
  suggested by the Department of Electrical Engineering and orally
  present another academic paper in English at an international
  conference. The said publication and presentation of academic
  papers should be completed while the author is still studying at
  the Department of Electrical Engineering. In addition, the said
  papers should include the full name of National Central
  University and designate the author's thesis advisor as a co-
  author. Except for the author's thesis advisor (including the
  author's co-advisor), the author who publishes or presents the said
  papers must be the first author of the papers so that the
  Department of Electrical Engineering would admit that the
  papers are the author's research results produced during the
  author's studying at the Department of Electrical Engineering.
  The list of suggested academic journals and international
  conferences may be decided by each division. After each division
  produces its own list, the list will take effect upon approval by
  the Academic Committee.
  (2) In addition to the quality and quantity of a student's publication
  and presentation of academic papers, whether the student's course
  taking is appropriate or whether the course credits he or she has
  earned are sufficient should also be reviewed. The student will
  become a PhD candidate right after he or she passes the review.
  3. A student should pass the preparatory oral defense of his or her
  graduation thesis.
  (1) The preparatory oral defense of a student's graduation thesis
  should be convened by the student's thesis advisor. The thesis
  advisor should gather three faculty members working at NCU
  with the title of associate professor, assistant researcher, or
  above to form a Preparatory Oral Defense Committee. The
  three faculty members should also have the qualifications
  required by NCU for being appointed commissioner of the
  committee. Besides the thesis advisor, at least one
  commissioner should be a faculty member at the student's
  division to review the student's graduation thesis and to decide
  whether the content of the thesis meets the requirements of
  completeness and coherence.
  (2) A student must gain approval from at least two-thirds of the
  commissioners present to pass the preparatory oral defense.
  Only after the student passes the preparatory oral defense can
  the oral defense of his or her graduation thesis be arranged.
  Furthermore, the manuscript of the student's graduation thesis
  along with his or her publications should be publicly displayed
  at the office of the Department of Electrical Engineering.
  (3) The commissioners of the Preparatory Oral Defense Committee
  must serve as ex officio commissioners of the Oral Defense
  Committee. If any ex officio commissioner needs to be
  replaced with another person due to any unexpected incidents,
  the replacement should first obtain approval from the to-be-
  replaced commissioner. Upon approval by the said
  commissioner, the replacement should be reported as a special
  case to the Academic Committee for review and approval. The
  replacement can be granted if the Academic Committee
  approves it.
  (4) If a student fails to pass the preparatory oral defense, he or she
  must wait three months to apply for taking the second
  preparatory oral defense, and the commissioners of the retake
  must be the same ones from the first preparatory oral defense.
  If any commissioner needs to be replaced with another person
  due to any unexpected incidents, the replacement should first
  obtain approval from the to-be-replaced commissioner. Upon
  approval by the said commissioner, the replacement should be
  reported as a special case to the Academic Committee for
  review and approval. The replacement can be granted if the
  Academic Committee approves it.
  4. A student must pass the degree examination. The degree examination
  is held in the form of an oral examination. A videoconference can be
  adopted upon approval by the director of the Department of Electrical
  Engineering if any unexpected incidents take place. Matters not
  covered by this paragraph should be subject to National Central
  University Regulations on the Degree Examinations for Postgraduate
  Students.
Article 5  Matters not covered by the Regulations should be handled per relevant
  rules and regulations stipulated by the Ministry of Education and NCU.
 Article 6  The Regulations have been approved at the department affairs meeting
  and verified at the academic affairs meeting prior to their
  implementation. Any future amendments should follow the same
  administrative procedures.
Note: A doctoral student should first apply to his or her division for the review of his or her graduation qualification;
after his or her graduation qualification passes the review, the result of the review should be submitted to the
Academic Committee for another review. (Resolution of the 2nd department affairs meeting of the Department of
Electrical Engineering in the 103rd academic year (1040310))
