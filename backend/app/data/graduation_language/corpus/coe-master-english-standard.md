# 國立中央大學工學院碩士班學生英文能力合格標準（115.06.10教務會議核備，中英對照）

國立中央大學工學院碩士班學生英文能力合格標準
English Proficiency Standard for Postgraduate Students in the Master’s Program
  of College of Engineering, National Central University
 Passed by remote meeting of College Administrative Meeting between May
  20 ~ 23, 2021  110 年 05 月 20-23 日院行政會議通訊審議通過
 Approved for future reference by the Academic Affairs Committee on June
  23, 2021  110 年 06 月 23 日教務會議核備
  Amended by the College Affairs Committee on March 1, 2024  113 年 03 月 01 日院務會議通過
Approved for future reference by the Academic Affairs Committee on March
  20, 2024  113 年 03 月 20 日教務會議核備
  Amended by College Administrative Meeting on March 10, 2026  115年03月10日院教評暨院行政會議通過
  Approved for future reference by the Academic Affairs Committee on June
  10, 2026  115年 06月 10 日教務會議核備
第一條 為積極培養學生之英文能力，以提升本院碩士班學生國際移動力及職涯競爭力，爰
  訂定本標準。
Article 1. This Standard was stipulated to cultivate the English proficiency of students as well as
  enhance the international mobility and professional competitiveness of students from the
  Master’s program of this College.
第二條 實施對象：本院 110 學年度起入學之碩士班學生適用。
Article 2. Target: Applies to all postgraduate students in the Master’s program of this College
  admitted from the 2021 academic year onwards.
第三條 本院碩士班學生於申請學位考試前須達下列其一之英文檢定測驗分數，且不限取得
  檢定之時間。
Article 3. Students in the Master’s program of this College must achieve one of the following
  English proficiency test scores before applying for their degree examination. There is no
  restriction on when the proficiency test was conducted.
  如未達英文檢定測驗分數者，得憑取得入學資格後，參加英文檢定測驗之成績證明，修
  習一學期本院規定之碩士班進修英文課程(每學期開學前公告本課程相關資訊)， 且
  成績達 70(含)分以上，視為通過。
  If a student fails to meet the English proficiency test score requirement, they may use the
  transcript from the English proficiency exam they took after being accepted by the NCU
  and take one semester of the Remedial English for Master’s Program course specified by
  this College (course information will be announced before the start of each semester). A
  score of 70 or higher will be treated as a pass.
  一、  TOEIC：600 分
  二、  TOEFL ITP：483 分
  三、  TOEFL iBT：64 分
  TOEFL iBT：3.5級【2026成績計分制度更新(自2026/01/21起實施)】
  四、  IELTS：5.0 級分
  五、  全民英檢中高級初試：143 分
  六、  培力英檢(聽讀測驗)：170 分
  1. TOEIC: 600 points
  2. TOEFL ITP: 483 points
  3. TOEFL iBT: 64 points
  TOEFL iBT: Level 3.5 [2026 TOEFL Scoring Update (Effective January 21, 2026)]
  4. IELTS Band Score: 5.0
  5. General English Proficiency Test (GEPT) - High-Intermediate Grade: 143 points
  6. BESTEP (listening and reading comprehension test): 170 points
第四條 各系所如自行訂定更嚴格之英文檢定測驗標準，從其規定。
Article 4. If more rigorous English proficiency test standards are set by each Department/Graduate
  Institute then their rules prevail.
第五條 如有特殊情形者，得由各系所依個別情況，專案處理。
Article 5. Special circumstances can be dealt with by each Department/Institute on their own on a
  case by case basis.
第六條 本標準如有未盡事宜，悉依教育部及本校相關規定辦理。
Article 6. Matters not specified in these Standards shall be processed in accordance with other
  relevant regulations of the MOE and NCU.
第七條 本標準經院級相關會議審議通過，送教務會議核備後實施，修正時亦同。
Article 7. These Standards were passed by the Institute Affairs Committee and submitted to the
  Academic Affairs Committee for approval before implementation. Revisions shall follow
  the same procedure.
