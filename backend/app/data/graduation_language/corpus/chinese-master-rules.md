# 國立中央大學中國文學系碩士班研究生修業辦法（112學年度入學適用，中英文版）

第一條 本辦法依據本校「博士班、碩士班研究生學位考試細則」訂定。
第二條 修業年限與學分
  一、本系碩士班修業年限以二至四年為限。
  二、畢業應修總學分數最低為27學分。
  修課說明：
  (一)須修滿本系及認可之外校系至少 27 學分(含本系必選課程)，其中外校系學分至
  多 9 學分，所修課程須經指導教授同意，若尚無指導教授，則由系主任決定。
  (二)學分抵免依本系「研究生學分抵免辦法」辦理。
第三條 申請學科考試資格
  一、須滿足前項課程與學分要求，當學期可修滿27學分者可提出申請。
  二、參加系上學術活動至少九次，及校內外相關學術之研討會至少四次。
  三、外籍生、僑生及陸生非中文相關科系畢業者，經審查後得要求補修大學部課程學
  分。
  四、滿足上述要求者，始具有學科考試之申請資格，另定本系「碩士班研究生學科考
  試辦法」。
  學科考試每科以二次為限，必須於提出學位考試前一學期結束前通過，未通過者應予
  退學。
第四條 學位論文開題報告
  研究生於通過論文結構與內容評鑑審查後，即可著手撰寫論文，另定本系「研究生學
  位論文開題報告實施辦法」。
第五條 學位考試
  通過學科考試與學位論文開題報告後，依學位考試相關規定提出論文，申請論文口試。
第六條 本辦法如有未盡事宜，依教育部及本校相關規定辦理。
第七條 本辦法經本系系務會議通過，並報教務會議核備後實施，修訂時亦同。

Article 1:These regulations are established in accordance with the university's "Regulations on
  Degree Examinations for Doctoral and Master’s Programs."
Article 2: Duration of Study and Credits
  1. The duration of the master's program in this department is limited to two to four years.
  2. The minimum total number of credits required for graduation is 27 credits.
  Course Requirements:
  (1)A minimum of 27 credits must be completed, including mandatory and elective courses
  from this department and approved courses from other departments or universities. Up
  to 9 credits may be from other departments or universities, subject to the approval of
  the advisor. If no advisor is assigned, the department chair will decide.
  (2)Credit transfer is handled according to the department's "Graduate Credit Transfer
  Regulations."
Article 3: Application for Qualification for Subject Examination
  1. Students must meet the course and credit requirements mentioned above and may
  apply if they are expected to complete 27 credits in the current semester.
  2. Participation in at least nine departmental academic activities and at least four related
  academic seminars, both internal and external to the university, is required.
  3. International students, overseas Chinese students, and students from Mainland China
  who did not graduate from Chinese-related programs may be required to take
  additional undergraduate courses after review.
  4. Only those who meet the above requirements are eligible to apply for the subject
  examination. The department's "Master's Program Subject Examination Regulations"
  apply.
  Students may take each subject examination a maximum of two times. The examination
  must be passed by the end of the semester preceding the application for the degree
  examination. Students who fail to pass the examination will be required to withdraw from
  the program.
Article 4: Thesis Proposal Defense
  After passing the evaluation of the thesis structure and content, students may begin
  writing their thesis, in accordance with the department's "Implementation Guidelines for
  Graduate Thesis Proposal Defense."
Article 5: Degree Examination
  After passing the subject examination and thesis proposal defense, students may submit
  their thesis and apply for the oral defense in accordance with the relevant degree
  examination regulations.
Article 6:Matters not covered by these regulations shall be handled in accordance with the relevant
  regulations of the Ministry of Education and the university.
Article 7:These regulations shall be implemented after being passed by the Department Affairs
  Meeting and approved by the Academic Affairs Meeting. The same procedure applies to
  any revisions.
