Article 1 The Regulations are formulated according to Academic Regulations, National Central University
  Regulations on the Degree Examinations for Postgraduate Students.
Article 2 The academic duration of Master’s degree is according to the university's regulation.
Article 3 Graduation Requirements of Economic Class:
  (a) The minimum number of credits required for graduation is 30. Required subjects and elective
  subjects are listed in the attached table.
  (b) 16 credits of required subjects shall be taken.
  (c) At least 3 credits of required elective subjects shall be taken.
  (d) At least 11 credits of elective subjects shall be taken. If any students are unable to take the courses
  of this Master’s degree, they may take courses of similar nature from other departments with the
  approval of the Director of this Department.
  (e) Graduate students who are returning to school may choose to follow the regulations at the time of
  enrollment or the academic year when they resume school.
Article 4 Graduation Requirements of Law Class:
  (a) The minimum number of credits required for graduation is 31. Required subjects and elective
  subjects are listed in the attached table.
  (b) 16 credits of required subjects shall be taken.
  (c) At least 3 credits of required elective subjects shall be taken.
  (d) At least 12 credits of elective subjects shall be taken. At least 6 credits of elective subjects must
  be collected from courses offered by faculty members at the institute. they may also take courses
  of graduate institute of law in University System of Taiwan or similar nature from other
  departments with the approval of the Director of this Department.
  (e) Graduate students who are returning to school may choose to follow the regulations at the time
  of enrollment or the academic year when they resume school.
Article 5 The selection of faculty advisor shall be in accordance with the following provisions:
  (a) Graduate students should select a faculty advisor before participating in the oral presentation of
  the thesis proposal, register at the office of the institute and submit the faculty advisor

  also go to the office to register the change.
  (b) If the faculty advisor of graduate student is not a full-time professor in the institute, he or she
  should find a co-advisor who is full-time professor in the institute; if the faculty advisor is a
  professor from another school who is not a part-time professor in this institute, the faculty advisor
  should obtain the approval of the institute's executive meeting.
  (c) The number of students can be supervised by faculty advisor are regulated in the attached table.
Article 6 Graduate students should write a master's thesis and pass the thesis oral examination. Applicants who
  apply for thesis oral examination should complete the following procedures in advance:
  (a) Make oral presentations of thesis proposal (once in Chinese and English) and the first draft of
  the thesis arranged by the institute. The presentation is held once each semester.
  (b) Applicants for the thesis oral examination must apply at least one week before the thesis oral
  examination date, and the application must not exceed the deadline set by the school.
  (c) Have passed the English threshold regulated by the Implementation Rules of the School of
  Business Administration of National Central University for Taking English Courses for the
  Master’s Program.
Article 7 Graduation qualifications for graduate students should meet the following provisions:
  (a) Meet the Graduation Requirements.
  (b) Write a master's thesis and pass the degree examination. The degree examination shall be
  conducted orally in accordance with National Central University Regulations on the Degree
  Examinations for Postgraduate Students.
Article 8 Matters not provided for under the Regulations shall be governed by relevant requirements of the
  Ministry of Education and this University.
Article 9 The Regulations shall be deliberated and adopted by the Institute Affairs Meeting and shall be
  implemented following verification with the Academic Affairs Meeting, and this shall apply to
  amendments thereto.

(1) Resolution on the number of students to be mentored in the master’s class:
  (a) Our full-time professors or part-time professors can each supervise at most three students, and no

Institute Affairs Meeting, the number of students to be advised may be increased as appropriate.
