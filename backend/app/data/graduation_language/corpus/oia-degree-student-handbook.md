  and Arrival
  System used in Taiwan: GSM System
  Where to get pre-paid card: Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport
  Purchasing a cellphone (or a phone card) upon arrival is strongly recommended. A
  cellphone is the ideal line of communication should emergencies arise. Acquiring a

  Insurance
  Safety Insurance for NCU Students
  All NCU students are covered by Safety Insurance, which is included in the
  tuition bill.
  NHI Enrollment for Students with a Valid ARC
  Students with a valid ARC are eligible for the NHI program after living in
  Taiwan for 6 months. This includes:
  6 months of continuous residence, or
  A single trip abroad of no more than 30 days, with the days abroad deducted from the
  residency period.
  Once eligible, OIA will assist international students in enrolling in the NHI
  program. The fee will be included in the tuition bill.
  Upon arrival in Taiwan, students cannot immediately apply for NHI. Instead, they will be covered
  by Cathay Insurance for the first few months at NCU.
  Cathay Insurance
  Cathay Insurance is provided for international students who do not have NHI.
  With this insurance, you can visit clinics or hospitals and pay the full fee
  upfront. The reimbursement coverage is as follows:
  Item  Insured Value
  (Maximum)  Remark
  Clinic  NTD 1,000/day or visit  If the insured is hospitalized due to illness or injury, the company
  will reimburse the following expenses
  Hospitalized  NTD 1,000/day  The benefit payment will not exceed NTD120,000/hospitalization
  Reimbursement for outpatient/emergency treatment will not exceed NTD1,000/visit.
  To apply for reimbursement, please submit the following documents to OIA:
  Receipt  Medical certificate (obtained from the hospital/clinic)
  Post office account book, passport, and ARC (only for first-time applicants)
  Application form (available at OIA)
  OIA will submit the documents to Cathay Insurance for processing the medical subsidy

Weather
 Taiwan has a subtropical climate. In Zhongli, temperatures range from 28–35°C
 in summer and 8–15°C in winter. Spring and fall are the most pleasant seasons,
 except for the “Plum Rain Season” in May and June, which lasts about a month.
 For detailed weather updates, visit Central Weather Bureau
