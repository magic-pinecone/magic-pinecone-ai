  Chapter I General
1.  This Rule is promulgated in accordance with the “Regulations for Degree Examination for
  Graduated Students in PhD and Master's Programs”.
  Chapter II Requirements for Enrolling
2.  A PhD program student has to choose a dissertation advisor within six months of the first day
  of enrolling. The advisor is limited to the full-time professors in this Institution.
3.  Any PhD program student has to be accepted by his/her advisor before he/she takes courses or
  quits school. If the advisor has not been determined, the Institution director has to be
  responsible for it.
4.  During the period of preparing the dissertation, a PhD program student shall not change his/her
  dissertation advisor at his/her discretion. Under any circumstance, change of an advisor is
  considered necessary, a written notice to his/her original advisor and application with the
  Academic Committee is required and can be changed only if the Committee has reviewed it
  and approved.
5.  A PhD program student must mandatorily register “Books and Newspaper Seminar” (0 credit)
  for two semesters and “Special Topic Seminar” (0 credit) for four semesters during his/her
  studying period. In addition, he/she must mandatorily register “Dissertation Writing Skill for
  Digital Learning” (3 credits) before he/she graduates. Also, the mandatory courses include the
  following four categories:
   Prospective Digital Technology: has to register one course in Department of
  Computer Science and Information Engineering.
   Assessment for Digital Learning and Cognitive and Neuroscience Science: has to
  register one course in Graduate Institute of Learning and Institution OR one course
  in the Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience.
   Designation for Digital Learning: has to register at least two courses in the Institute
  of Network Learning.
  Any student who registers the above courses has to get their advisor’s consent and
  signature before his/her registration. For any difficulty in course selection, the Courses
  Committee has to arrange proper courses.

8.  Pass the Annual PhD Learning Evaluation of this Institution
  Any PhD program student shall submit the annual PhD learning evaluation report before the
  end of academic year during his/her studying year (the graduation year is not included). The
  report shall be evaluated by his/her advisor and another teacher with a level higher than
  assistant professor.
  Chapter III Qualifying Examination
9. The PhD qualifying examination can be divided into two categories, mandatory and optional.
  Students have to pass the two tests, no matter which one goes first, and are required to pass the
  qualifying oral test of this institution. The mandatory test is an oral test for research literature
  of digital learning. The content of literature will be announced at the beginning of each
  semester. The optional test is one of the three items: (1) Research literature of digital learning
  oral test and the content will be announced at the beginning of each semester. (2) Publishing a
  high quality periodical essay or presenting a full paper in an internal conference. The student
  shall be the first author, except his/her advisor, and this essay will not be considered as the
  graduation dissertation. (3) Performing a practice-type research plan and report content of the
  plan in an oral test.
10. Any PhD program student is eligible to apply for a qualifying test as soon as he/she attends the
  program, is required to attend one or more test before the end of the third semester, and must
  pass the mandatory and optional tests within the first six semesters. Any student who cannot
  pass the PhD qualifying test will be requested to leave school in accordance with the “National
  Central University Regulations”.
11. The qualifying test will be held once in each semester and each student is eligible to take the
  mandatory and optional test no more than three times.
  Chapter VI Comprehensive Examination
  I Application with presentation of dissertation
12. Pass the oral test of the PhD research plan in this Institution
  (1) The oral test of the PhD student’s research plan will be reviewed by a team containing
  his/her advisor and at least two associate professors invited by his/her advisor. The team
  will review the feasibility for the student’s research plan for his/her PhD dissertation.
  (2) Any student who fails to pass the first oral test of his/her PhD research plan has to apply
  for the second oral test within one year of his/her failure. When passing the review of the
  research plan, he/she will become a PhD candidate.
  (3) After passing the oral test of the PhD research plan, the student can only apply for the
  reviewing procedure of qualification for graduation after another semester.
13. Pass the literature review held by the Academic Committee in this Institution
  (1) Any PhD program student’s dissertation shall contain at least one essay being published in
  full text in any top global academic journal. The essay shall be published in the name of
  the Online Learning Technology Institution during his/her studying and the student has to
  be the first author, except his/her advisor. After doing so, this institution can admit his/her
  research results during his/her studying.
  (2) The student’s PhD dissertation and published essay has to be delivered to the Academic
  Committee for review. The scope of review includes if courses registered are proper and if
  the credits are satisfied. The student has to register more courses if it is considered
  necessary after review.
14. Pass a Pre-oral Test for graduation dissertation in this Institution
  The Pre-oral Test is held by the student’s advisor and at least four associate professors invited
  by the advisor. The team will review if the dissertation is comprehensive and has a logical
  connection.
15. Pass the Degree Examination
  The Degree Examination is held in accordance with the “Rules Governing Degree Examination
  of the PhD Program and Master’s Program”, and the composition of oral test members and at
  least half of the members are professors.
16. Each student can apply for Degree Examination in each semester. Any PhD program student
  who has applied for a degree examination and cannot complete the Degree Examination during
  the semester for any cause has to withdraw his/her application of Degree Examination with the
  university by the end of semester according to the school calendar. Failure to withdraw or
  attend examination will be deemed as failure of the examination.
  II Applications with industry-university cooperative research projects
To push forward industry-university cooperation, the PhD program student may conduct research
with an industry-university cooperation institution and team after he/she enrolls in the program and
applies for graduation with an :industry-university cooperative: research project. The procedure is as
follows:
17 Pass the performance of practical-type research plan and attend the oral test to report the
  contents of the plan.
18 The oral test of the PhD program student’s research plan will be reviewed by a team containing
  his/her advisor, an associate professor invited by his/her advisor, and researcher from the
  cooperation institution. The team will review the feasibility of the student’s research plan for
  his/her PhD dissertation. When passing the review of research plan, he/she will become a PhD
  candidate.
19  Pass the literature review held by the Academic Committee in this Institution
  (1) Any PhD student has to present his/her dissertation at a global conference in relation to
  his/her research topic and his/her dissertation shall be presented in a global conference
  with a reputation. The student shall be the first author except his/her advisor. The
  dissertation shall be a full review and be published in the name of the Online Learning
  Technology Institution during his/her studying. After doing so, this institution can admit
  his/her research results during his/her studying.
  (2) Completing the PhD dissertation and his/her contribution has been recognized by his/her
  advisor and industry-university cooperation company in the advisor’s recommendation
  letter.
  (3) The student’s PhD dissertation and essay published has to be delivered to the Academic
  Committee for review. The scope of review includes if courses registered are proper and if
  the credits are satisfied. The student has to register more courses if it is considered
  necessary after review.
20. Pass a Pre-oral Test for the graduation dissertation in this Institution
  The Pre-oral Test is held by the student’s advisor and at least one researcher from the
  cooperation institution and at least three associate professors invited by the advisor. The team
  will review if the dissertation is comprehensive and is with logical connection.
21. Pass the Degree Examination
  The Degree Examination is held in accordance with the “Rules Governing Degree Examination
  of PhD Program and Master Program”, and the composing of oral test members and at least
  half of members are professor.
22. Any unsettled affairs can refer to Section 1 of Chapter VI regarding Degree Examination for
  further implement.
  Chapter V Graduation and Leaving School
23. A student has to publish a dissertation with certain specifications and submit the same before
  his/her graduation.
24. Before a student leaves the school, he/she has to clean the spaces and equipment used in the
  research room and lab, return all equipment and stuff borrowed, have his/her advisor,
  institution office, and director sign on the leaving school application form, then he/she can start
  to process the leaving procedure.
  Chapter VI Others
25. Any unsettled affairs shall follow regulations of Ministry of Education and this university for
  further handling.
26. The Rules was passed by the Institution Affairs Council and reviewed and approved by the
  Academic Affairs Meeting. The same as it is amended.
