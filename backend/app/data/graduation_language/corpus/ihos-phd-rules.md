# 國立中央大學地球科學學院水文與海洋科學研究所博士班研修辦法 Academic Guidelines for Ph.D. Program

國立中央大學地球科學學院
  水文與海洋科學研究所博士班研修辦法
Graduate Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences
at National Central University
Academic Guidelines for Ph.D. Program
  Approved at the Institutional Conference on Oct. 28, 2005  94.10.28 所務會議通過
  Approved for reference at the Academic Affairs Committee on Mar. 28, 2006  95.3.28 教務會議核備
  Revised and approved at the Academic Affairs Committee on Oct. 17, 2007 96.10.17 教務會議討論修正
Approved for reference and approved at the Academic Affairs Committee on Dec. 26, 2007 96.12.26 教務會議核備通過
  Revised at Institutional Conference on Mar. 19, 2010  99.3.19 所務會議修正
Approved for reference and approved at the Academic Affairs Committee on Jun. 23, 2010  99.6.23 教務會議核備通過
  Revised at Institutional Conference on Sep. 18, 2013  102.9.18 所務會議修訂
  Approved at the Academic Affairs Committee on Oct. 16, 2013 102.10.16 教務會議核備通過
  Revised at Institutional Conference on Mar. 9, 2016  105.3.9 所務會議修訂
  Approved at the Academic Affairs Committee on Jun. 15, 2016 105.6.15 教務會議核備通過
第  一  條 本所為便於辦理學生之入學、研修需求、資格考試及學位論文考試等事宜，參照
  本校教務章則及相關規則，訂定本所博士班研修辦法（以下簡稱本辦法）。
  The guideline has been proposed based on the Academic rules and regulations of
  National Central University in order to provide the entrance, research, qualify exam
  and thesis of students of Graduate Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences at
  National Central University.
第  二  條 獲有碩士學位（或具同等學力），經本校博士班入學考試通過者；或學士班應屆
  畢業生（含申請提前畢業學生）及碩士班研究生修業一年後申請逕修讀博士學位，
  經本所所務會議審查通過，校長核定逕修讀博士者。
  Applicants, who have obtained a master degree or equivalent degree, pass the Ph.D
  entrance examination. Applicants who have bachelor degree or master students with
  one year studied apply for direct admissions into doctoral program should be verified
  at the institute conference and then proved by the President.
第  三  條 博士班入學考試由本所招生試務工作委員會辦理。
  The Ph.D. entrance is processed by the admission committee members of Graduate
  Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences.
第  四  條 入學考試方式與成績計算，依本校研究所招生簡章之規定辦理。
  The manners and calculating results of Ph.D. entrance is processed based on the
  admission prospectus of National Central University.
第  五  條 入學考試錄取名單，應經本所招生試務工作委員會審查通過後，報請學校招生委
  員會公告。
  The admitted list of entrance examination will be announced by committee of
  admission of National Central University after proved by the admission committee
  members of Graduate Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences.
第  六  條  學生擬逕修讀本所學位者，應符合本校學生逕修讀博士學位辦法規定（學士班應
  屆畢業生含申請提前畢業學生及碩士班修業一年以上），於每年七月十五日（第
  一梯次）或一月十五日（第二梯次）前向本所提出申請。
  Applicants who apply for direct admissions into doctoral program should conform to
  the Guidelines Governing Direct Admissions into Doctoral Programs at National
  Central University, which specifies students obtain bachelor degree including advance
  the date of graduating or one or more year master students, and apply to Graduate
  Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences before July 15 (first schedule) or
  January 15 (second schedule).
第  七  條  逕修讀博士學位申請之審查，應經本所所務會議同意通過後送教務處呈請校長核
  定。
  Applications for direct admissions into doctoral program should be sent to the office
  of Academic Affairs after verified at the institutional conference, and it will be proved
  by the president.
第  八  條  學生應請本所專任（案）教師任指導教授。本所合聘或兼任教師每屆至多指導一
  名學生，且須與所上專任（案）教師共同指導。學生入學後由指導教授督導並查
  核其研修進度。研修期間可申請更換指導教授，經所務會議通過得更換之。
  Students should be advised by the formal faculties of Graduate Institute of
  Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences. Adjunct and joint appointment faculty can advise
  one student each year with formal faculty. Students should be supervised the research
  progress by the advising professors after entrance. Students could apply for changing
  advising professors, and it’s proved after passed at the institutional conference.
第  九  條  學生應遵循本所研究規定及學校有關規定進修，如有違反規定之情形，應由指導
  教授提請所務會議處理。
  Students should follow the guidelines of this Institute and regulations of the school to
  study. Advising professors should ask for the institutional conference to cope with if
  students violate the guidelines and regulations.
第  十  條  學生入學後應於第三學期結束前與指導教授共同擬訂研修計畫書，並邀請二至三
  位助理教授（含）以上或相當資格之研究人員組成委員會審查其研修計畫書。期
  限前未提研修計畫書或研修計畫書經審查未通過者，須說明原因，並申請延期，
  經所務會議通過後得延期至多一學年。未照章辦理者，經所務會議通過後得更換
  指導教授。
  Students should finish the study plan under the guide of advising professors before the
  end of third semesters, and the study plan should be examine by the examination
  committee which includes two to three teachers who acted as assistant professors or
  qualified researchers. The reasons should be explained and applied to extend while
  the study plan is not submitted or is unapproved by the committee. The extended
  deadline is one more semester at most after proved at the institutional conference. If
  the advising professors do not guide the study plan as the guideline, the advising
  professors can be replaced after proved at the institutional conference.
第 十 一 條 學生之研修計畫書至少應包括二至三門核心科目、研究重點、研修進度、著作發
  表及各項考試資歷等資料。
  The study plan of students should at least include two to three core subjects, research
  points, progress, publications and the examination experiences and qualifications.
第 十 二 條 學生應於博士班資格考前至少一學期通過研修計畫書審查，未通過者，不得申請
  博士班資格考試。
  The study plan of students should be approved at least one semester before Ph.D.
  Qualification Examination. Ph.D students can not apply for Qualification
  Examination when the study plan is not approved.
第 十 三 條 學生於本所修業期間，除專題討論與書報討論之學分外，至少須修滿 18 學分，
  逕修讀博士學位者，則至少須修滿 34 學分。
  Students are required to take at least 18 credits for normal students and 34 credits for
  direct admissions into doctoral program students. This required credits are exclude the
  credits of seminar and journal study.
第 十 四 條 學生可在本所或相關系所開設之課程中選修，其中可作為博士班核心科目之課程
  由本所課程委員會決定之。
  Students can select courses in this and other related graduate institutes. The core
  subjects for Ph.D students from these courses are decided by the course committee
  members of this Institute.
第 十 五 條 學生於就讀碩士班階段修習通過本所訂之博士班核心科目課程或類似課程者，得
  向本所課程委員會申請免修。
  Ph.D students who had studied the core subjects or related subjects of Ph.D program
  during master degree could apply for deducting credits to the course committee
  members of this Institute.
第 十 六 條 學生在學前四學期必修本所博士班專題討論與書報討論。博士班三年級以上免修
  前述兩門課程，惟每學期須於書報討論課程時段報告研究進度一次。
  Students should take required seminar and journal study for the first four semesters,
  and these two courses are not required after third grade. Students are requested to
  report research progress in journal study of each semester.
第 十 七 條 博士班研究生修業期限以二至七年為限，在職進修研究生未在規定修業期限修滿
  應修課程或未完成學位論文者，得再延長修業年限一年。
  Students are required to complete the degree from two to seven years. If part-time
  doctoral students cannot finish the required courses or degree thesis in allotted time,
  the study will be extended one year at most.
第 十 八 條 博士班資格考試包括筆試與口試。
  The Qualification Examination of Ph.D program includes Written and Oral.
第 十 九 條 博士班資格考筆試於每學期開學前舉行一次，欲參加考試者，須於十二月或六月
  以前向本所提出申請。
  The Qualification Examination of Ph.D program is held once before every semester
  began. Applicants should apply to this Institute before December or June of each year.
第 二 十 條 學生所提研修計畫通過審查者於該學期結束後，經指導教授同意得申請參加資格
  考筆試。博士班研究生應於第三學年結束前參加資格考試，未參加者須說明原因
  並申請延期，經所務會議通過後得延期至多一學年。至第五學年結束前（不含休
  學）仍未通過博士班資格考試者，應依「國立中央大學學則」第六十條規定報請
  學校退學。
  After the semester, which the study plan had passed, is finished and having approval
  by the advising professor, students can apply for attending the Written Qualification
  Examination. Ph.D student should attend Qualification Examination before the third
  year of Ph.D program is over. If not, students should explain the reasons and apply for
  delaying the date. This date can be extended one year at most after proved at the
  institutional conference. Students will be quitted school based on the Article 60 of
  National Central University of Study Regulations in case of Ph.D students have not
  passed the Written Qualification Examination until the end of fifth year (not including
  suspension of schooling).
第二十一條 資格考筆試為二門考試科目，其範圍、題目與評分由博士生指導委員會依該生所
  修課程及博士論文方向決定之。考試科目應與該生之博士班核心科目相關。
  The Written Qualification Examination includes two subjects, which are connected to
  the core subjects of the Ph.D students. The ranges, titles, and grades of these two
  subjects are decided by the advising committee members, who follow the courses and
  Ph.D thesis of the students.
第二十二條 博士生指導委員會為指導教授邀請三至五位助理教授（含）以上或相當資格之研
  究人員所組成，指導委員會委員資格須經所長審查通過。
  The three to five advising committee members, who are assistant professors or
  qualified researchers, of the Ph.D students are invited by the advising professor. The
  qualification of the committee members are approved by the director.
第二十三條 資格考各科筆試成績以 70 分（含）以上為及格（滿分為 100 分）。
  The passing grade for each subject of the Written Qualification Examination will be
  70 and full grade will be 100.
第二十四條 學生未通過資格考筆試者（不滿 70 分），應於半年內申請重考。資格考以二次
  為限，二次未通過者應依「國立中央大學學則」第六十條規定報請學校退學。
  Students should apply for reexamining in half year if the grade of Written
  Qualification Examination is lower than 70. The number of times for Qualification
  Examination is two times limited. Ph.D students who don’t pass the Qualification
  Examination for two times will be quitted school based on the Article 60 of National
  Central University of Study Regulations.
第二十五條 學生通過資格考筆試後，應於一年內提出論文研究計畫書，並由博士生指導委員
  會負責審查並進行口試。
  Students should submit the thesis research proposal within one year after passing the
  Written Qualification Examination. The thesis research proposal and Oral
  Qualification Examination are judged by the advising committee members.
第二十六條 學生論文研究計畫書未通過審查者，應於半年內重新提請審查。提請審查以三次
  為限，三次未通過者應依「國立中央大學學則」第六十條規定報請學校退學。
  Students should apply for reexamining the research proposal within half year if it
  doesn’t pass. The number of time for reexamining is three times limited. Ph.D
  students who don’t pass the reexamination for three times will be quitted school based
  on the Article 60 of National Central University of Study Regulations.
第二十七條 學生通過資格考試並符合「國立中央大學博士班、碩士班研究生學位考試細則」
  規定者，經博士生指導委員會同意，得為博士學位候選人。
  Students, who pass the Qualification Examination, conform the “National Central
  University Rules Governing the Master’s and Doctoral Degree Qualifying Exam and
  Thesis Defense” and be approved by the advising committee members, will be
  regarded as a Ph.D candidate.
第二十八條 未通過博士生論文研究計畫或博士資格考試者，若為逕修讀博士學位研究生者，
  依「國立中央大學學生逕修讀博士學位辦法」規定辦理，其餘學生依「國立中央
  大學學則」規定辦理。
  Ph.D students, who fail in examining the thesis research proposal or the Qualification
  Examination, will be processed by the “Guidelines Governing Direct Admissions into
  Doctoral Programs at National Central University” for direct admissions into doctoral
  program students, but other students are processed by “National Central University of
  Study Regulations”.
第二十九條 學生應於學位論文考試之前，須以第一作者至少發表（含正式被接受者）一篇於
  修業期間完成且與博士論文研究有關之期刊或專書論文。
  Students should release at least one paper as first author, including formally accepted,
  before thesis defense, and it should be released in journal or books chapters, which
  are related to the doctoral thesis research.
第 三 十 條 學生符合研修要求、資格考試或其他相關規定，經指導教授同意，得向本所提出
  學位考試之申請。
  Students, who are confirmed with the study regulations, Qualification Examination, or
  other regulations and approved by advising professor, are eligible to apply for Ph.D
  defense.
第三十一條 學位考試由博士生指導委員會依據「國立中央大學博士班、碩士班研究生學位考
  試細則」辦理學位考試。
  The Ph.D defense is held by the advising committee members based on the “National
  Central University Rules Governing the Master’s and Doctoral Degree Qualifying
  Exam and Thesis Defense”.
第三十二條 學位考試每學期舉行一次。已申請學位考試學生，若因故無法於該學期內完成學
  位考試，應於本校校曆規定學期結束日之前報請本校撤銷該學期學位考試之申
  請，逾期未撤銷亦未舉行考試者，得以一次不及格論。
  The Ph.D defense is held once per semester. Candidates, who applied for degree
  defense, should cancel the defense before the end date of that semester in case of
  candidates cannot finish the defense. There will record fail one time when the degree
  defense don’t cancel on time and hold.
第三十三條 學位論文的評分標準由博士生指導委員會決定之。
  The standards of grading of Ph.D defense is decided by the advising committee
  members.
第三十四條 逕修讀博士學位研究生，若通過博士學位候選人資格考試，但未通過博士學位考
  試，得依據「國立中央大學學生逕修讀博士學位辦法」第八條規定，授予碩士學
  位。
  Candidates, who are Direct Admissions into Doctoral Programs and pass the
  Qualification Examination but fail in the Ph.D defense, will obtain Master degree
  based on the Article 8 of “Guidelines Governing Direct Admissions into Doctoral
  Programs at National Central University”.
第三十五條 畢業前應依規定格式製作論文、提要之電子檔案，於離校前一週繳交。
  Candidates should submit regulation thesis and summary in electronic files to school
  one week before leaving.
第三十六條 離校前應清理研究室及實驗室所使用之空間及設備，歸還所借用之各項設備及物
  品，離校申請單須經指導教授、所辦公室、各實驗室及所長簽名確認後，始可向
  學校辦理離校手續。
  Candidates should clean up the space and equivalents in the office and laboratory and
  return goods borrowed from school before leaving. The application form for leaving
  school should be confirmed and signed by advising professor, front office of this
  Institute, each laboratory and director before submit to school.
第三十七條 本辦法如有未盡事宜，依教育部及本校有關規定辦理。
  In case this regulation does not involve, procedures follows the related regulations of
  Ministry of Educations and National Central University.
第三十八條 本辦法經所務會議通過，並送教務會議核備後實施，修正時亦同。
  This regulation is proved at the institutional conference, and approved at the
  Academic Affairs Committee Meeting, as well as correction.
