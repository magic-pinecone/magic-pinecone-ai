Remarks
It takes about 1-2 weeks working days to review and issue the permit.
Work Permit is valid for at most 1 year:
International students applying for a work permit from October 1 of the first semester to September 30 of the following year cannot have their permit extend beyond September 30 of the next year. However, those who provide a certificate of enrollment are not subject to this restriction.
Except during summer and winter vacations, the maximum working hours are 20 hours per week.
If you obtained a work permit through the school during your studies, it will automatically become invalid after applying for graduation, suspension, or withdrawal. Please do not work illegally to avoid violating the law.
