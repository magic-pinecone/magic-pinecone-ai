# TEEP Program

TEEP Program
Taiwan Experience Education Program
Program Introduction
Aims to attract international students for short-term study and internships in Taiwan, foster collaborative research with university faculty, and enhance the international visibility of Taiwan’s higher education.
Visit TEEP Official Website
<https://teep.studyintaiwan.org/>
Program Details
Application Process: The Ministry of Education invites applications from universities every year. Interested faculty can apply through the TEEP website. The 2025 application period is expected to start around November (subject to confirmation). The OIA will notify all campus units accordingly.
TEEP Student Scholarship
Other expenses, such as airfare and accommodation, are not covered and must be paid by the student. If the participation period is less than one month, the scholarship will be prorated based on the number of days. The total monthly scholarship and administrative allowance per student shall not exceed NT$15,000. The maximum funding period per student is generally six months (180 days).
Laboratory Administrative Fee
The hosting professor may retain up to 20% (NT$3,000 per person per month) of the funds for project-related administrative expenses.
Eligibility
Overseas Chinese youth with at least a high school diploma; doctoral degree holders are ineligible.
Not eligible for those who have studied or researched in Taiwan before, including exchange students or degree students.
Must enter Taiwan for TEEP; foreign spouses or workers already in Taiwan are ineligible.
