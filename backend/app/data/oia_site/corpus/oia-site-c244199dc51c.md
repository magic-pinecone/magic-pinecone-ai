# Incoming Dual Degree Programs

Incoming Dual Degree Programs
NCU accepts students nominated by the International Offices of its partner universities. For the eligibility of application, please contact the International Offices of your home university regarding the partnership with NCU.
Procedure of Application
01
Apply for the Dual Degree Student Program from Home University
02
Nominated by the International Office of Home University
03
Documents Sent to Host University
04
Host University Examines the Application
05
Result Informed by Host University
06
Applicants Receive "Letter of Acceptance" or "Letter of Rejection"
Required Documents
Application Form (Download .odt)
<https://oia.ncu.edu.tw/static/file/26/1026/img/144/ApplicationFomforDDupdated.odt>
Official Academic Transcript (current study) issued by Home University
Official Enrollment Certificate (current study) issued by Home University
Official Degree Certificate of the Highest Degree
Official Transcript of the Highest Degree
Letter of Nomination issued by the International Affairs Office of Home University
Letter of Recommendation
Copy of Information Page of Passport
Head Photo (JPEG file and follow the passport photo standard)
NCU Student Dormitory Application Form (optional)
Other required documents according to the double degree agreements
Timeline & Deadlines
Application Deadline
Fall Semester (First Semester)
May 15
Spring Semester (Second Semester)
November 15
Duration of the Semester
Fall Semester
August ~ January

Spring Semester
