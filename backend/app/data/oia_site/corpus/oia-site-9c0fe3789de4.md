# Ambassador

NCU Goodwill Ambassadors
Mission & Background
Officially established in 2007, the NCU Goodwill Ambassadors is a student organization under the Office of International Affairs at National Central University.
Our Objectives:
1. To foster meaningful interactions and cultural exchanges between domestic and international students.
2. To assist the university in advancing international affairs, providing hospitality, and offering campus guiding tours during international delegations' visits.
To comply with the Extracurricular Activities Division regulations allowing venue bookings 30 days prior to events, the group officially registered as a student club in 2015.
Training Programs
Our comprehensive training includes international etiquette, posture and grooming, public speaking, and campus guiding tours in both English and Chinese.
We invite professionals from the International Protocol and Goodwill Association, professional makeup artists, and English language instructors to conduct workshops on grooming, makeup application, reception protocols, and social etiquette. These courses enable our members to apply their skills effectively while presenting the professional, elegant, and welcoming image of a Goodwill Ambassador.
Organizational Structure
Led by one President and one Vice President.
Event Planning Division and Media & Publicity Division: Composed primarily of probationary members.
Marketing Division and Public Relations & Professional Development Division: Composed of official members.
Each division is managed by a designated division head.
