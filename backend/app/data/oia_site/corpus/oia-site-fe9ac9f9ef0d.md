Notes:
Applicants enrolling with foreign academic certificates are required to submit the following additional documents:
A. One photocopy of the foreign academic degree certificate (the copy must be authenticated with an official verification stamp issued by an ROC
overseas mission).
For degree certificates issued by institutions in non-English-speaking countries, a Chinese or English translation of the degree certificate is also required.
The translation must be authenticated with an official verification stamp issued by an ROC overseas mission or notarized by a local district court or a
certified notary public in Taiwan.
B. One photocopy of the official academic transcript for all years of study obtained abroad (the copy must be authenticated with an official
verification stamp issued by an ROC overseas mission).
For transcripts issued by institutions in non-English-speaking countries, a Chinese or English translation of the transcript is also required. The translation
must be authenticated with an official verification stamp issued by an ROC overseas mission or notarized by a local district court or a certified notary
public in Taiwan.
C. An original Certificate of Entry and Exit Records issued by the National Immigration Agency, Ministry of the Interior, covering the periods of entry
and exit corresponding to the duration of study for the foreign academic degree. (This requirement is waived for foreign nationals and overseas Chinese
students.)
