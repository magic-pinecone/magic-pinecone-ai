新型專班產學獎助金 INTENSE program Scholarship
新型專班之獎學金來源包括行政院國家發展基金提供之「產學獎助金」及合作企業提供之
「生活津貼」，細項詳列如下：
The scholarships for these two programs are sourced from the "Industry-Academia Subsidy"
funded by the National Development Fund of the Executive Yuan and the "Living Allowance"
provided by partner companies. The details are as follows:
(1) 產學獎助金：由行政院國家發展基金提供
Industry-Academia Subsidy: Funded by the National Development Fund of the Executive Yuan.
A. 學生初次來臺的必要行政費用：
Initial Necessary Administrative Expenses for First-Time Arrival to Taiwan:
採一次性補助，包含來臺前的健康檢查費用、簽證費用及文書驗證費用。
One-time subsidy covering the costs of pre-arrival medical examination, visa fees, and
document verification fees.
以地區分列補助上限為 The subsidy limits by region are as follows:：
(a) 新南向區域國家及其他國家上限新臺幣 10,000 元。
New Southbound countries and other countries: up to NTD 10,000.
(b) 歐美區域國家上限 25,000 元。
Europe and the Americas: up to NTD 25,000.
(c) 檢據核銷，核實報支，並以收據開立當日之臺灣銀行美金對新臺幣匯率計算。
Expenses shall be reimbursed based on actual receipts, and the amount will be calculated
using the USD to TWD exchange rate of Bank of Taiwan on the date the receipt is issued.
B. 學生初次來臺單程機票 One-Way Airfare to Taiwan：
採一次性補助，以來臺最直接航程之經濟艙單程機票。
One-time subsidy based on the cost of an economy-class one-way ticket for the most
direct route to Taiwan.
以地區分列補助上限為 The subsidy limits by region are as follows:：
(a) 新南向區域國家及其他國家上限新臺幣 9,000 元。
New Southbound countries and other countries: up to NTD 9,000.
(b) 歐美區域國家上限 35,000 元。
Europe and the Americas: up to NTD 35,000.
(c) 檢據核銷，核實報支，並以收據開立當日之臺灣銀行美金對新臺幣匯率計算。
Expenses shall be reimbursed based on actual receipts, and the amount will be calculated
using the USD to TWD exchange rate of Bank of Taiwan on the date the receipt is issued.
C. 註冊入學後最多 2 年的學雜費 Tuition and Miscellaneous Fee Up to 2 years：
(a) 依學生實際應繳交給學校的學雜費給予補助，一學期上限為新臺幣 50,000
元，每年補助上限新臺幣 100,000 元。
Subsidy covers actual tuition and miscellaneous fees that the student is required
to pay to the school, with an annual cap of NTD 100,000 (NTD 50,000 per semester).
(b) 學生入學第一年給予學雜費補助。第一年第 1 學期及第 2 學期，學生班級排名須
在前 70%或學期平均成績達 80 分(含)以上；倘未達上述成績標準，第二年第 1 學期
即不得領取學雜費補助。第二年第 1 學期前，英文授課班級之學生須通過華語文能
力測驗 (TOCFL)基礎級(Level 2，相當於 CEFR A2)，聽、讀 兩項皆須達 A2 級(含)以上。
另須通過學校與合作企業共同進行審查成績與表現後，擇優核給學雜費補助。倘未
通過華語能力測驗者，第二年第 2 學期即不得領取學雜費補助。
Tuition and miscellaneous fee subsidies are provided during the first year of enrollment.
Students must rank in the top 70% of their class or achieve an average grade of 80 or above
in both the first and second semesters of their first year; students who fail to meet these
academic requirements will not be eligible for tuition and miscellaneous fee subsidies in
the first semester of the second year. Before the beginning of the first semester of the
second year, students in English-taught programs must achieve at least Level A2 in both
listening and reading on the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language (TOCFL, Basic Level 2,
equivalent to CEFR A2). In addition, students must pass performance evaluations jointly
conducted by the university and its partner company. Based on the evaluation results,
tuition and miscellaneous fee subsidies for the second year will be granted on a merit basis.
Students who fail to meet the required TOCFL level will not be eligible for tuition and
miscellaneous fee subsidies in the second semester of the second year.
(2) 生活津貼：由合作企業提供。
Living Allowance: Funded by the partner company.
每位學生每月至少 1 萬元。第二年需經合作企業評核通過後核發，並需依合作企業要
求進行實習。
Each student will receive at least NT$10,000 per month. In the second year, the allowance will
be granted only upon passing the evaluation by the partner company and participation in an
internship as required by the company.
(3) 領取國發基金產學獎助金學生的義務：
Obligations for Receiving Industry-Academia Subsidy:
A. 領取國發基金產學獎助金的學生，依據領取年限具有相應留臺就業年限的義務。
Students who receive the Industrial-Academic Subsidy from the National Development
Fund of Taiwan are obligated to work in Taiwan for a duration equivalent to the number
of years they received the subsidy.
B. 領取 1 年產學獎助金者，具有 1 年(12 個月)留臺就業義務，領取 2 年產學獎助金者，
具有 2 年(24 個月)留臺就業義務。
For students who received the subsidy for 1 year, there is a 1-year obligation to work in
Taiwan. For those who received the subsidy for 2 years, there is a 2-year obligation to
work in Taiwan.
(4) 屬可歸責於學生之原因，應繳還國發基金產學獎助金：
Repayment of Industrial-Academic Subsidy Due to Attributable Reasons:
A. 就學期間因個人因素中途退出專班，如申請轉學、轉系、休學返國，經學校輔導後
仍放棄繼續就讀專班、或經學校依學則退學、開除學籍等情形，學生應全額返還已
領之產學獎助金。
If a student discontinues the specialized program due to personal reasons during the
study duration (such as transferring to another school or department, taking a leave of
absence to return home), and if they withdraw from the program despite counseling from
the school, or if they are dismissed or expelled due to school regulations, they must repay
the full amount of the scholarship received.
B. 學生學習表現不佳，未通過學校及企業評核標準，並經學校輔導後仍無改善且依學
則處以退學、開除學籍等情形，學生應全額返還已領之產學獎助金。If a student’s
performance fails to meet the standards of both the school and the partner company, and
after counseling, they still fail to improve and are dismissed or expelled according to
school regulations, they must repay the full amount of the scholarship received.
C. 學生畢業後選擇不至合作企業就業，並經學校輔導後仍無改善者，學生應全額返還
已領之產學獎助金。
If a student chooses not to work in the partner company upon graduation and does not
improve after counseling, they must repay the full amount of the scholarship received.
D. 學生畢業就業後違反公司規定被依法終止勞動契約，並經學校輔導後仍無改善者，
學生應依未就業之月數比例返還產學獎助金；不滿一月者，以一月計。
If a student, after beginning employment, violates company regulations and is lawfully
terminated, and does not improve after counseling, they must repay the scholarship in
proportion to the remaining months of the employment obligation; any incomplete
month will be rounded up to a full month.
E. 學生畢業後至合作企業就業期間未滿受領產學獎助金年限(領取 1 年產學獎助金者
須留臺就業 12 個月，領取 2 年產學獎助金者須留臺就業 24 個月)：應依其未就業
之月數比例繳還產學獎助金;不滿一月者，以一月計。
Students who do not fulfill the required employment period at the partner company after
graduation must repay the received industry–academia scholarship in proportion to the
number of months not worked. A partial month will be counted as a full month.
若因中途轉換工作，考量尋職所需時間：
參加 2 年計畫者，於履約日起算，應於 2 年 6 個月內完成留臺就業 2 年之義務；
若未就業時間累計超過 6 個月，則應依其未就業之月數比例償還產學獎助金。
參加 1 年計畫者，於履約日起算，應於 1 年 6 個月內完成留臺就業 1 年之義務；
若未就業時間累計超過 6 個月，則應依其未就業之月數比例償還產學獎助金。
For job transitions, considering the reasonable period needed for job searching:
Students participating in the two-year program must complete their two-year
employment obligation within 30 months from the contract commencement date. If the
accumulated period of unemployment exceeds six months, they must repay the
scholarship proportionally to the number of months not employed.
Students participating in the one-year program must complete their one-year
employment obligation within 18 months from the contract commencement date. If the
accumulated period of unemployment exceeds six months, they must likewise repay the
scholarship proportionally to the number of months not employed.
但合作企業有勞動基準法第十一條第一項第一款至第四款或第十四條第一項規定
情形之一者，學生得免繳還已受領之產學獎助金。
However, if the partner company falls under any the circumstances specified in Article 11,
Paragraph 1, Subparagraphs 1 to 4, or Article 14, Paragraph 1 of the Labor Standards Act,
the student shall be exempt from repaying the Industry-Academia Subsidy already
received.
