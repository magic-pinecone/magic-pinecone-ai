# VISA and ARC.pdf

VISA/ARC(Alien Resident Certificate)
About Visitor Visa
It’s short-term visa. Those who stay in Taiwan for less than 180 days should apply for
Visitor Visa. For degree students who hold Visitor Visa to enter Taiwan, they should
apply for Resident Visa before the expired date of Visitor Visa. Exchange students
who apply for one-semester program should have visitor visa, the duration can be
extended in Taiwan.
About Resident Visa
It’s long-term visa. Those who stay in Taiwan for more than 180 day should apply for
Resident Visa. Exchange students who apply for one-year program can either have
Visitor Visa or Resident Visa to enter Taiwan.
About ARC
Resident Visa holders should apply for ARC at local service center of National
Immigration Agency within 15 days.
