The NCU Career Center supports students in building workplace competencies and
discovering their ideal career paths. We offer a three-stage model focused on:
1. Career Exploration and Awareness
2. Career Learning and Experience
3. Career Planning and Application

we help you unlock your strengths and prepare for a confident career future.

Established in 2009, the Campus Recruitment Team is
a student-run organization under the guidance of the
Career Center. Structured into five divisions—
Executive, PR, Secretariat, Marketing, and Design—the
team operates like a matrix organization, emphasizing
both vertical and horizontal coordination.
Through hands-on roles in event planning, corporate
outreach, design, promotion, and logistics, students
gain valuable hard and soft skills. The team serves as
a bridge between students and industry, easing their
transition into the workforce. Students from all
departments are welcome to join and contribute their
creativity to campus talent recruitment events.

CAREER-READY TRAINING
Held twice a year, these events include the Spring Campus Talent Recruitment
Sessions (March–May) and the Autumn Campus Talent Recruitment Sessions
(September–October).
Dozens of leading companies are invited to host sessions tailored to students’
needs. Company representatives come directly to campus to present industry
insights, hiring criteria, and future workforce needs. These sessions provide
students with a valuable opportunity to interact with professionals, understand job
openings, and get a firsthand look at real-world expectations.
For companies, it’s also a chance to build brand awareness and attract top talent.
Students often gain early interview opportunities and valuable career advice
directly through these sessions.
Company Info Sessions
The Career Fair is the highlight of the Spring recruitment season
(March-May), where over 100 companies are invited to campus to
set up recruitment booths. This large-scale event serves as a
platform for students to explore job opportunities, learn about
industry trends, and even participate in on-site interviews with
hiring managers.
Students not only gain direct access to company representatives
and current job openings but also stay informed on the latest
workplace developments—all without leaving campus. For many,
the Career Fair is the gateway to landing their first job offer.

Tailored Campus Recruitment Events
Customized Company Info Sessions
Companies can apply to host customized presentations based on
their internal hiring schedules. These sessions allow companies
to interact with students personally, clearly communicate job
openings, company culture, and talent needs. Time is also set
aside for Q&A and networking to improve the match between
students and employers.
CAREER-READY TRAINING
Special Recruitment Events
Through creative formats such as mobile experience trucks, coffee
trucks, on-the-spot interviews, and interactive games, these events
create engaging spaces for company-student interaction. Companies
can effectively convey recruitment needs while students enjoy an
approachable and dynamic job-seeking experience.
Internship Matchmaking Sessions / Information Events
Corporate Internship Info Sessions
Held periodically each semester, these sessions aim to help students gain early
exposure to the workplace. Both public-sector internship program briefings and
corporate recruitment visits are offered, allowing students to understand career
expectations and sometimes even secure interviews directly during the session.
